About COXY

Celebrating the Coastal Beauty of Bangladesh.

COXY is a bespoke destination discovery guide crafted to celebrate the sublime marine ecology and coastal geography of Cox's Bazar. From world-famous beachfront sweeps to untouched barrier islets and forested mountain roads, our mission is to inspire thoughtful exploration and appreciation for Bangladesh's premier shoreline.
